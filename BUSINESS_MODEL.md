# GREWECO Business Model

## Core Concept

**GREWECO is a marketplace platform that sells NFT-ized paulownia trees to developers and companies for ecological impact and ISO compliance.**

## Business Flow

### 1. Tree Listing
- Plantations are created with paulownia trees
- Trees/hectares are listed for sale on the platform
- Each tree/hectare can be sold as an NFT

### 2. Purchase Process
- **Developers** buy trees for:
  - Ecological projects
  - Building certifications
  - CO₂ offset for construction projects
  
- **Foreign Companies** buy trees for:
  - ISO 14001 (Environmental Management)
  - ISO 14064 (Greenhouse Gas Accounting)
  - ISO 14067 (Carbon Footprint)
  - Corporate sustainability goals
  - Investment/ESG compliance

### 3. NFT Certificate Issuance
- Upon purchase, buyer receives:
  - NFT certificate (blockchain token)
  - CO₂ absorption certificate
  - ISO compliance documentation
  - Ownership proof

### 4. Value Proposition
- **For Developers**: Ecological credentials for buildings
- **For Companies**: ISO compliance, carbon credits, ESG reporting
- **For Platform**: Revenue from tree sales, ongoing monitoring fees

## Key Entities

1. **Tree/Plantation** - The asset being sold
2. **Buyer** - Developer or Company purchasing trees
3. **Purchase/Order** - Transaction record
4. **NFT Certificate** - Ownership and CO₂ proof
5. **ISO Compliance Record** - Standards compliance tracking

## Revenue Model

- Tree/hectare sales (one-time)
- Annual monitoring fees
- Certificate renewal fees
- ISO compliance reporting fees
